Simulation rules

  • Capital: 100,000 virtual PLN from 6 Nov 2026. This is a simulation; no real orders are placed.
  • Where positions come from: the ranking table of the daily report (swing). We take classes A and B with a total score of at least 60, R to target 1 of at least 1 and to target 2 of at least 2, no quarterly results within 7 days, at most 5 new orders per day.
  • Entry: a limit order at the upper edge of the entry zone, valid for 5 sessions. It fills at the lower of the open and the limit if price traded down to the limit. If the session opens below the stop, the order is cancelled.
  • Position size: one position costs at most 5,000 PLN (whole shares). Shares priced above 5,000 PLN each (e.g. LPP) are skipped. Profits from sales return to cash; a loss at the stop reduces it. When all cash is committed to positions and orders, new orders wait until a position is closed (target or stop).
  • Exits: stop (at the open price on a gap), target 1 (sell half, move the stop to the entry price), target 2 (the rest), or after 30 days at the session close.
  • Daily candles: when one candle touches both stop and target, the stop counts (pessimistic assumption). On the entry day only the stop is checked.
  • Costs: 0.2% per transaction. Capital gains tax (19%) is not included.
Screening exercise under the given framework, not investment advice.
