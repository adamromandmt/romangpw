Market and macro

Wall Street, 5 Nov: S&P 500 −4.36%, Nasdaq Composite −5.52%, Dow Jones −3.44%

Closes on 5 Nov: Dow Jones 51,523.06 (−3.44%, −1,833 points), S&P 500 7,353.73 (−4.36%), Nasdaq Composite 24,315.0 (−5.52%; more than 10% below last month's record), Russell 2000 2,729.51 (−4.46%). Broadcom and Nvidia fell about 9% each, Oracle 7%, and the iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) more than 10% (its worst day since March 2020). WTI crude (December) rose 2.86% to USD 90.64 per barrel, Brent (December) 4.12% to USD 98.52. Budimex: Veolia selected the consortium's offer for the Łódź Energy Recovery Plant (PLN 712.63 m net)

Veolia Nowa Energia selected, as the most advantageous, the offer of a consortium of Budimex (leader, 49.2% share) and Keppel Seghers Belgium (50.8%) for building the Łódź Energy Recovery Plant. The tender was run by Veolia Energia Polska on behalf of Veolia Nowa Energia. The offer is worth PLN 712.63 m net and EUR 109.25 m net, with completion within 36 months of contract signing. This is a selection of an offer, not a signed contract; the article gives no date for signing.

Why it matters: concerns BDX; until the contract is signed, it is not part of the order backlog.

Source: StockWatch.pl (information from Budimex) · 6 Nov, 14:47

Elektrotim: two contracts with PGE Energia Odnawialna worth PLN 331.6 m net in total

The PLN 254.4 m net contract covers the supply of storage units and MV/LV transformer stations for four storage facilities (MEE Lotnisko, MEE Ścieki, MEE Wojciechowo, MEE Pelplin), due by 29 Feb 2028. The second, PLN 77.2 m net, covers installation, electrical and ICT works, due by 30 Dec 2028. Elektrotim's 2025 consolidated revenue was PLN 580 m (data from the article).

Why it matters: concerns ELT; the combined value of the contracts is about 57% of 2025 consolidated revenue, spread over delivery until the end of 2028.

Source: StockWatch.pl · 6 Nov, 13:27
