Method and caveats

  • Quotes: GPW archive in the repository, 155 companies with a 12 Nov session (excluding KVT, no turnover since 9 Nov), roughly 260 sessions of history; raw prices, not adjusted for dividends. Indicators computed in Python from the CSVs (EMA20, SMA50/100/200, Wilder RSI14, MACD 12/26/9, ATR14, 52-week high, volume / 20-session average).
  • Global context gathered by sub-agents (WebFetch/WebSearch): US, VIX, UST and commodities for 11–12 Nov (some items only from 11 Nov, USD/PLN from 9 Nov); the 12 Nov Russell 2000 and VIX closes, EUR/PLN, new rating decisions and US PPI were not verified. Primary sources (PAP Biznes, Bankier, Parkiet) were unavailable or blocked; I did not work around the blocks. Two sources gave different US levels for 11 Nov (Weekend Spread: S&P 7,626.55); I used Yahoo/AP, consistent with Capp Notes.
  • Fundamentals (Fund/30), quarterly results and consensus for the banks, KTY, CDR, BDX, PCO and LPP come from the 10 Nov screen and were not refreshed today; I refreshed only Allegro (watchlist). I found no new ESPI filings for ranked companies from 12–13 Nov (attempted through search engines and agents, without access to filing lists).
  • Report dates: confirmed in the Strefa Inwestorów calendar only for CAR (17 Nov), GPW (16 Nov), KGH (18 Nov), PZU (19 Nov), BFT (19 Nov), ALE (12 Nov); CDR 24 Nov, LPP 3 Dec, ACP 26 Nov carried over from the 10 Nov screen; PCO unverified.
  • Tech/70 and price levels were adjusted for the 12 Nov move; R is calculated from the middle of the entry zone (PKO T1 from a Fibonacci retracement, CDR and LPP T1 from ATR, KTY and MBK T2 from consolidation height).
  • I did not check 13 Nov quotes (pre-market report); entry levels are limit orders and an opening gap may change them.
