Indicators computed in Python from CSV (EMA20, SMA50/100/200, Wilder RSI14, MACD 12/26/9, ATR14, 52-week high, volume/20-session average). Screen: 20-session turnover above 8 m zł, price above SMA200, up to 22% below the 52-week high; 22 companies passed the filter, manual assessment. `suspect_jumps_over_30pct` entries (CRJ, LBW, ODL, TRN, WAS) do not concern ranked stocks.
- **External sources:** direct fetching of the Strefa Inwestorów and StockWatch home pages failed (access request unanswered); individual Strefa Inwestorów articles of 18.11 (PAP Biznes) could be read and are the source of: global context (Europe, US, VIX, yields, oil, metals, Nvidia result, Fed, MF auction), results of Cyfrowy Polsat, Inter Cars, WP, Synektik and Bogdanka, and GPW intraday quotes of 18.11. **Global data come from one outlet, without a second source and without US index closing levels for 18.11 (only the 17.11 closes are known); they are not an official close.** The search engine also returned headlines not confirmed in the text, which were omitted. **Not verified:** KGHM results (published after the 18.11 session), USD/PLN, EUR/PLN, Polish CPI, MPC calendar, ESPI releases of ranked companies for 18–19.11, earnings calendar (as of 16.11). No blocks were circumvented.
- **Fundamentals (Fund/30)** were not refreshed: carried over from the scans of 16–18.11, which refreshed quarterly results of PKO, PEO, KTY, DNP, BDX, CDR, PZU, ING; for GPW SA and PGE fundamentals are unverified (Fund/30 lowered as a precaution). Consensus, valuation and revisions not verified.
- Entry levels are limit orders; an opening gap on 19.11 (including after the US session and Nvidia's result) may change them. T2 for PKO is a projection (record plus about 1.2 ATR), and T1/T2 for the others come from retracements, 52-week highs, ATR and range height. Fund/Tech and classes are an analytical assessment under the scanner's framework.

Screening exercise under the given framework, not investment advice.
