### EU: preliminary agreement on 121m additional free ETS allowances
On Thursday evening EU negotiators agreed 121m additional free CO2 emission allowances, of which 116m for industry until 2030; the saving for industry is more than EUR 10bn at EUR 80–90 per tonne. The Modernisation Fund will receive 5m allowances (European Parliament proposal: 11m), conditionally. Poland could receive about EUR 14bn (about PLN 60bn) from it in 2021–2030. The agreement requires approval by the European Parliament and the Council.

**Why it matters:** it concerns industrial and chemical companies on GPW (including ATT, KGH, PKN) through the cost of emission allowances.
