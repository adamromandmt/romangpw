### MPC: the Senate accepts nominations for two seats on the Council until 25 November
Senate Marshal Małgorzata Kidawa-Błońska set the window for nominating candidates to the Monetary Policy Council (RPP) from 9 to 25 November 2026 (until 12:00). The Senate is to appoint two members to replace Ludwik Kotecki and Przemysław Litwiniuk, recalled on 6 Nov after resigning in connection with their appointment to the NBP Management Board. Under the NBP Act the successors must be appointed no later than three months after the recall. MPC member Gabriela Masłowska said the latest NBP projection argues for a rate hike in January, but that it is better to wait until early 2027 for clarity on regulated prices.

**Why it matters:** the composition of the MPC decides NBP rates; it concerns the banking sector (net interest income) and the zloty.

### JSW: loan agreement with ARP for up to PLN 1.066bn, repayment by 30 June 2038
Jastrzębska Spółka Węglowa signed a loan agreement with the Industrial Development Agency (ARP) for up to PLN 1.066bn, disbursed in tranches. The interest rate is WIBOR 3M plus a margin (the margin was not disclosed); principal is to be repaid quarterly after a grace period until 30 June 2029, and the financing runs until 30 June 2038. The first funds will repay a PLN 400m advance with interest, received under the preliminary agreement of 9 March 2026 to sell stakes in JZR and Przedsiębiorstwo Budowy Szybów; returning the advance automatically terminates that agreement. The rest is to finance operations during the JSW Group Restructuring Programme. Later tranches depend, among other things, on completing restructuring tasks and on the consent of the financing consortium; collateral includes mortgages and pledges on mine assets and pledges on JSW KOKS shares.

**Why it matters:** concerns JSW; the loan replaces proceeds from the planned sale of subsidiaries and ties disbursements to restructuring progress.
